# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members and contributors pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and inclusive in language
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at **b.lourdhuraju1234@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly.
